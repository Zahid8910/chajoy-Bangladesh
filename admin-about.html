A little more about us

Made for a little more joy.

ChaJoy is a Bangladeshi drink and refreshment destination serving freshly prepared tea, milk tea, boba, shakes, sundaes, and ice cream. We make it easy to find a favorite drink, share a break with friends, or take something refreshing with you.

Freshly prepared

Our drinks and desserts are prepared at the branch so customers can enjoy them fresh and made to order.

Something for everyone

Explore fruit teas, classic teas, milk tea, boba, shakes, sundaes, and ice cream across our menu.

Find your ChaJoy

Visit your nearest branch for dine-in or takeaway service, and enjoy a comfortable stop whenever you need a refreshment.
